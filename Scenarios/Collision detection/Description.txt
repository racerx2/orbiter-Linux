Test scenarios for the CollisionDetection add-on: reentry breakup, and
vessel-to-vessel contact against the actual mesh triangles.

These need two things switched on before they do anything:

  Launchpad -> Modules    -> CollisionDetection
  Launchpad -> Parameters -> Vessel -> "Damage and failure simulation"

The damage setting is greyed out once a session has started, so it has to be
ticked on the Launchpad. Both are reported in Orbiter.log at session start.
